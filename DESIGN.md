## Brand & Style

This design system defines a high-performance combat apparel and custom sublimation manufacturing ecosystem (MMA, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Muay Thai, No-Gi Grappling). It merges the technical discipline of high-contact combat sports with an ultra-refined, editorial direct-to-consumer digital commerce aesthetic. 

### Brand Personality & Tone
- **Technical Precision & Mastery:** Inspired by the disciplined craft of athletic tailoring, reinforced stitching, and ergonomic muscle-mapping. The tone avoids loud, hyper-aggressive MMA tropes in favor of restrained authority, architectural poise, and elite athletic standard.
- **Bespoke Craftsmanship:** Conveys enterprise manufacturing reliability alongside boutique atelier care—catering both to solo grapplers buying premium gear and fight academies/gyms ordering custom team kits.
- **Visual Style:** Minimalist editorial structure meets technical athletic commerce. Characterized by wide structural margins, high-density informational hierarchy, micro-pill badges, clean product grid cards with color-swatch indicators, and subdued sand/bone backdrops framed by deep carbon black and electric combat cyan accents.

## Colors

The palette establishes an organic athletic foundation with dark slate anchors and striking, focused combat accents.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#111417` - Deep Onyx Slate):** Acts as the foundational anchor for primary actions, heavy editorial typography, contrast hero blocks, and high-impact customizer containers.
- **Secondary (`#088395` - Combat Cyan / Deep Teal):** The functional highlight tone. Used for actionable inline badges, interactive customized nodes, live order workflow steps, cart indicators, and focus states.
- **Tertiary (`#C9A86A` - Champion Gold / Ochre):** Reserved for technical certification chips, IBJJF-compliance tags, championship badges, and rating stars.
- **Neutral (`#F4F1EA` - Warm Sandstone / Bone):** The dominant surface canvas. Replaces sterile cool white with a tactile, organic athletic mat/canvas tone that softens contrast while maintaining crisp readability.

### Semantic Tones & Accents
- **Canvas Base:** `#F4F1EA` (Warm Sandstone)
- **Card / Surface Tint:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) and `#EDE8DE` (Subtle Recessed Neutral)
- **Border / Divider Rule:** `#DFD9CC` (Fine Sandstone Border)
- **Text Primary:** `#111417` (Deep Onyx)
- **Text Secondary / Muted:** `#6B675E` (Warm Mineral Gray)
- **Success / Validated:** `#107C41` (Green Belt)
- **Alert / IBJJF Restricted:** `#C53030` (Mat Warning Red)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the technical discipline and subtle industrial edge of **Space Grotesk** for headlines with the clean, modern, high-legibility geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for interfaces, product cards, specifications, and editorial body narrative.

### Principles
- **Editorial Category Strips:** Navigation strips (e.g. `RASHGUARDS ↗`, `FIGHT SHORTS ↗`, `BJJ GIS ↗`) utilize `category-caps` with strict uppercase styling and subtle letter spacing.
- **Inline Feature Symbols:** Display headlines incorporate inline accent icons and pill tags (e.g., circular badges, globe icons, technical markers) to replicate the rich magazine-like layout structure.
- **Data & Dimension Labels:** Size indicators (`S`, `M`, `L`, `XL`), GSM weight indicators, and batch MOQ counters leverage strict uppercase, high-contrast medium labels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a flexible, structured grid that alternates between expansive editorial compositions and tight product modules.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Maximum content container width of `1360px` centered within a full-bleed viewport. Outer margins are `3.5rem` (`margin`) with `1.5rem` (`gutter`) between grid tracks.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Fluid container with `2rem` side margins and `1rem` column gutters.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** `1.25rem` side margins, tight `0.75rem` gutters. Product cards collapse into a high-density 2-column or stacked 1-column layout.

### Sectional Rhythm
- **Top Utility Rail:** Slim 36px marquee announcement strip (`#111417` background with `#F4F1EA` mono text) for batch manufacturing updates, discount codes, and global shipping notices.
- **Category Navigation Ribbon:** Horizontal scrolling or multi-column icon/preview category strip pinned directly below hero showcases.
- **Micro-Spacers:** Component-level spacing follows an 8px base rhythm (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 40px).

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows in favor of editorial surface layering, precision 1px structural outlines, and tactile micro-elevation.

### Elevation Strategy
- **Base Mat (Level 0):** The primary canvas color `#F4F1EA` sets a clean, low-glare organic mat surface.
- **Surface Elevation (Level 1):** Product cards, feature panels, and review blocks sit on crisp `#FFFFFF` or elevated `#EDE8DE` shapes wrapped in a single, fine 1px border (`#DFD9CC`).
- **Floating Overlays & Dropdowns (Level 2):** Modals, flyout carts, and custom 3D gear configuration pickers utilize an ambient low-opacity shadow tinted with the primary slate: `0 14px 34px -6px rgba(17, 20, 23, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(17, 20, 23, 0.04)`.
- **Hero Stacking:** Image and feature modules use offset layering—allowing imagery to sit flush within rounded containers with inset floating badge chips.

## Shapes

The design uses a balanced `Rounded` aesthetic (level 2). Radii provide friendly, tactile handling without diluting the angular strength of combat gear.

### Corner Radii Hierarchy
- **Micro Controls (`4px` - `6px`):** Swatch selectors, input counters, mini chips, IBJJF rank tags.
- **Base Components (`8px` / `0.5rem`):** Buttons, text fields, quantity selectors, and standard cards.
- **Large Surface Containers (`16px` / `1rem`):** Product showcase cards, testimonial panels, and delivery guarantee feature boxes.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Badges, category indicator tags, avatar outlines, and filter tabs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Cart / Customizer Launch):** Solid Onyx (`#111417`) fill, Warm Sandstone (`#F4F1EA`) bold text, `8px` corner radius, `44px` height. Hover transitions to Deep Slate `#22282E` with a micro `translateY(-1px)`.
- **Secondary / Highlight (Direct Order / Live Preview):** Combat Cyan (`#088395`) fill with pure white typography.
- **Ghost / Outlined:** Transparent surface, 1px `#111417` border, dark text.
- **Utility Buttons (Wishlist & Quick View):** Circular or compact rounded squares with subtle `#DFD9CC` border and pure white background.

### Product Grid Cards
- **Structure:** Clean vertical card composed of a top image container (`aspect-ratio: 4/5` or `1/1`), floating heart/cart hover icons, bottom metadata block with bold product title, price, and horizontal color/sublimation swatch dots.
- **Hover Micro-Interaction:** Subtle zoom on product preview, revealing secondary action drawer ("Quick Add" or "Customize").

### Category Navigation Strip
- **Anatomy:** Compact horizontal row featuring a micro thumbnail image, bold uppercase category title (e.g., `CUSTOM RASHGUARDS ↗`), and fine vertical dividing rules between items.

### Feature & Value Proposition Modules
- **Layout:** 4-column horizontal grid container housing minimal line-drawn athletic icons (e.g., "Reinforced Flatlock Stitching", "Anti-Microbial Fabric", "Worldwide Team Shipping", "IBJJF Legal Ranked Colors") enclosed in rounded 1px stroke pill containers.

### Customizer CTA & Workflow Section
- **Multi-step Order Visualizer:** Clean modular steps (`1. Upload Vector Crest`, `2. Select GSM & Cut`, `3. Approve 3D Render`, `4. Production & Delivery`) marked with clean numbered badge chips and cyan connection lines.
- **Interactive Attribute Selector:** Size buttons (`S`, `M`, `L`, `XL`, `2XL`, `Custom Fit`) styled with crisp square-pill borders that fill with `#111417` when active.

### Testimonial & Review Cards
- **Composition:** Asymmetrical 3-card layout mirroring the editorial reference—combining quote cards featuring large quotation marks (`“`), star rating clusters, user credentials (e.g., "Black Belt Instructor / Gym Owner"), and full-bleed action photo cards.
